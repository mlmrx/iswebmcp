# Current Product Hunt submission notes

Verified against Product Hunt's official launch and Help Center documentation on 2026-09-02.

## Listing constraints used by this kit

- Product name should contain the name only—no tagline, emoji, or extra description.
- Tagline is capped at 60 characters.
- Product Hunt's official pages currently disagree on the description limit: the launch guide says 500 characters, while the Help Center posting guide says 260. This kit's recommended description fits within 260 characters; the optional version stays below 500.
- Select up to three relevant topics.
- A square thumbnail is required; 240×240 is the recommended size and the file should stay under 3 MB.
- The gallery needs at least two images; 1270×760 is the recommended image size.
- A video is optional and must use a full public or unlisted YouTube URL—not a private video.
- An interactive demo is optional and must use a currently supported provider.
- Makers use personal Product Hunt accounts rather than a company profile.
- Add up to three genuine Shoutouts for products that materially helped the build.
- A substantive first maker comment is strongly recommended.

## Launch behavior

- Launching at 12:01 AM Pacific provides the full Product Hunt day, but launch only when the product and team are ready.
- Share the product organically and ask people to try it or join the discussion.
- Never ask for or incentivize upvotes, coordinate voting, mass-message strangers, or use automated engagement.
- Reply personally and substantively to the community.

## Featuring position

Product Hunt's featuring guidance emphasizes useful, novel, high-craft, live products and generally does not feature reports or directories. The isWebMCP listing therefore leads with its interactive utility: Quick Scan, Contract Workbench, Proof Lab, and 24 explorable patterns. WRI v1 is supporting evidence, not the product category.
